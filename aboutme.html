Why is horror important?

Why is horror so impactful? For one, it was one of the first genres of popular media to comment on the experiences of marginalized communities. While horror as a form of social critique began with the stereotypical characterization of BIPOC/disabled/queer people being portrayed as "scary" to advance the story of the white protagonist, it has evolved into a mirror for society's flaws regarding the treatment of those communities. For example, Jordan Peele's "Get Out" directly adressed gentrification and racial trauma, while the queer community often reclaims monsters as misunderstood survivors (the Babadook).

Also, There are no rules for storyteling in the horror genre, giving unlimited creative freedom to the screenwriter/director.
