Why is horror important?

So why is horror so impactful? For one, horror was one of the first generes of popular media to comment on the experiences of marginalized communities. While this area of horror began with problamatic origins (such as BIPOC/disabled/queer individuals being portrayed as "scary" to advance the story of the white protagonist) it has evolved into a mirror being held up to societies flaws regarding those communities. For example, Jordan Peele's "Get Out" directly adressed gentrification and racial trauma, while the queer community often reclaims monsters as misunderstood survivors (the Babadook).

Horror is also (in my opinion) one of the most creative genres to induldge in. There are no true rules, giving unlimited creative freedom.
